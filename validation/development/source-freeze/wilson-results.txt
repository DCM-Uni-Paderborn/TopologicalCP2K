Status

CP2K source tree. Both reproduce the historical evidence with the original
per-material MPI/OpenMP settings and the preselected numerical tolerances.
No reference values or tolerances were changed. The native Fortran Wilson
unit test and all 11 portable verifier tests pass.

Neon scalar-space diagnosis

The initial validation mistakenly used two OpenMP threads for Neon too. It
exceeded the fixed 1e-8 Wilson/link comparison threshold, despite retaining
the correct topological index. This failure and all control outputs remain
in the evidence archive.

The historical input uses five scalar states for second-variational SOC. At
Gamma, states 5 through 7 form a degenerate virtual triplet at approximately
10.90300406138 eV. The minimum separation above state 5 is 3.020e-14 eV.
Thus the scalar truncation includes only part of a degenerate multiplet.
Changing floating-point ordering can select a different component of it.

Two repeated one-thread runs reproduce the original five-state result at
roundoff. Repeated two-thread runs exhibit small variations. Including seven
scalar states (ADDED_MOS 3) completes the triplet and leaves a minimum scalar
cutoff separation of 3.25804611919 eV on the tested mesh. All 13 AO basis
states (ADDED_MOS 9) provide a full-scalar-space control. EXCLUDE_BANDS is
adjusted in both controls to keep the same eight occupied spinors.

Thirteen-state one-/two-thread differences in two repetitions
  Occupied eigenvalues:                 at most 2.416e-13 eV
  Wilson-eigenvalue chord:               at most 3.109e-15
  Link singular values:                 at most 1.333e-15

The 5-, 7-, and 13-state models all give Z2=0, but their precise spectra are
not identical. Scalar-space enlargement is a changed physical approximation,
not a reference-value correction. These controls do not demonstrate basis,
cutoff, or bulk material convergence. For a new robust demonstration fixture,
prefer the full 13-state scalar space; retain the old five-state data with
its provenance when discussing the historical figure.

Remaining publication work

This closes the fixed-source rerun gap for the two original Wilson figure
examples only. It is not the complete source/build freeze for the other
methods in the TopologicalCP2K manuscript. Their distinct archived code
snapshots, external-library licenses and source correspondence still require
the release review described by the separate repository audit. Integration
into the authoritative Overleaf sources and public deposition is not done.
The five-state Neon caveat should be included when preparing that update.
